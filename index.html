About Julia

I’m Julia, a software engineer and designer who enjoys creating products that feel clear, supportive, and thoughtfully crafted. I studied Computer Science with a minor in Graphic Design at Texas A&M University, which shaped the way I balance strong engineering foundations with calm, readable interfaces.

My work often centers on tools that help people make sense of their day-to-day lives or navigate complex information. Nightly began as a simple way to reflect and build healthier habits, while DingDuff grew from exploring how AI can organize dense legal text into something far more approachable.

I like designing software that reduces friction—whether that’s through clear data structures, smooth iOS interactions, or small visual details that make an experience feel gentle rather than overwhelming. I believe good tools should not only work well, but make people feel more grounded when they use them.

Outside of development, I draw and paint, and that creative background influences how I think about composition, hierarchy, and atmosphere in digital spaces. My goal across all my work is simple: build things that bring clarity, calm, and a sense of intention to whoever’s using them.

Experience

  • Nightly Reflections · iOS App

    Built and launched Nightly, a privacy-focused iOS journaling and sobriety support app. Led end-to-end product development including SwiftUI engineering, UX/UI design, local data persistence, and App Store deployment.

  • Plant Life · iOS App

    SwiftUI iOS application that gamifies plant care through a virtual currency system, enabling users to earn coins and customize their in-app living space.

  • DingDuff · AI Legal Assistant

    AI-powered workflow for lawyers that uses CourtListener to process long opinions and surface relevant case law. Worked on an developing backend features, validating AI outputs, and testing data retrieval pipelines for accuracy and reliability.

  • ANTISA · Forensic Nursing Web App

    Decision-tree web tool guiding nurses through diagnoses and documentation, worked with the Texas A&M forensic nurses for the capstone project.

  • CRM Insights

    The program scrapes the web for news related to companies in an asset manager’s mutual fund, assigns sentiment score and financial advisor’s clients’ exposure score.

  • Enigma · Narrative Game

    Ren’Py horror game with branching storylines and visual storytelling. Decisions in the game change the storyline, and open different characters and minigames.
